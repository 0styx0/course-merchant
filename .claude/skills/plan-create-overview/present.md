# Presenting the map

Used in step 6 of [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Bullets only, at most ~500 words, in this order so the owner can stop after the second section:

1. **Summary:** the goal in one bullet, the number of milestones, what is **Next**, what changed from the brief.
2. **Questions for you:** anything open that *blocks the split* (usually few).
3. **Brief:** the 5 bullets from step 1.
4. **Milestones:** each as its Status line, then *Needs*, *Size* and *Done when* on one line.
5. **Proposed decision rows:** the exact `| Decision | Why | Rejected |` text. For each rejected option: what it would give, cost and complexity. Only strong contenders.
6. **Open questions:** one line each, with the milestone and *blocks the split* or *can wait*.
7. **Out of scope.**
8. **Review result:** one line per accepted or rejected finding.
9. **Terms:** only those the owner may not know.
